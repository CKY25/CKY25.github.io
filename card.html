Booth sign & cards

Print on A4 at home: a booth sign plus a sheet of eight business cards with crop marks. Every QR code opens

Print-shop card file →

1. Booth sign

Tape it to your table or slide it into an A4 stand. Print in colour, "Fit to page" off.

2. Card fronts

Eight cards per sheet. Thick paper (250–300 gsm) works best. Cut on the crop marks.

3. Card backs

Feed sheet 2 back in and print this on the reverse, or use two-sided printing with "flip on long edge". The dark background runs past the cut lines, so a millimetre of misalignment won't show.
